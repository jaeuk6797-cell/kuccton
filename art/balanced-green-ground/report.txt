SUP — Balanced Warm sage planting surface

Base: origin/main d5b12a3cc59169cfdb7256927f436221ac0c1e80, fetched at task start.
Branch: codex/balanced-green-ground. Do not merge into main automatically.

Balanced Warm's planting surface now uses #91BD92 from the existing grass palette.
The unchanged grass retains its #67976D roots and #B8D9AC tips. Surface roughness
is 0.90, clearcoat/metalness/iridescence are zero, environment intensity is 0.20,
and the existing normal map uses 35% of its authored strength. Existing painted
luminance becomes restrained neutral variation (0.96–1.04), leaving the sage
material color in control while retaining depth and visible blades.

Isolation is by the actual Island_Surface mesh identity, never by material name
alone. The pond basin shares SUP Opalescent ground with the planting surface;
Balanced caches a separate surface material clone so that the pond keeps its
original material response. The custom opal reflection hook is skipped only on
the Balanced planting surface. All other meshes and theme paths are unchanged.

The original vertex color arrays are restored before each theme's paint is applied.
Balanced and Warm are now distinct paint states, preventing green carry-over on
direct switches between them. Material variants and the same geometry/color
buffers are reused. The additional cached surface material adds no draw call,
texture, geometry, rendering pass or expensive shader.

Protected assets and appearance:
  All GLB files, Blender source and generator remain byte-identical to d5b12a3.
  Pond, water, crystals, underside, metallic rim, flowers and trees are unchanged.
  Grass colors, geometry, placement, instance buffers and behavior are unchanged.
  Warm, Aurora and Original materials, shaders and vertex colors match baseline.
  Balanced remains the default; lighting and atmosphere were not edited.

  Grass: 1,000 clumps x 6 triangles = 6,000 triangles, one visible draw call.
  Total static island including grass: 20,099 triangles in every theme.
  Balanced uses one additional cached material to separate the surface from the pond.
