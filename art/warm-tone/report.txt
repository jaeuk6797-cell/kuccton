Visual result
The sky moves from lavender/aqua toward blush, peach and pale golden haze.
The ground keeps its opaque holographic opal response with a warmer pearl
body and pink/lavender reflected color. Champagne and rose-gold rims frame
warm quartz accents. Rose-brown mineral shading retains quiet aqua seams.
Procedural flowers, Blender peonies and trees share warm pearl/blush/lavender
materials. Warm-white key light and a pink-lavender fill replace the strong
cyan fill; one restrained aqua environment band preserves cool contrast.

Limits / unresolved verification
No functional defect was observed. Browser timing samples contain large frame
gaps during automated interaction/focus changes, consistent with throttling or
interruption. They are preserved in browser-comparison.json but are unsuitable
for an FPS or GPU regression claim. Draw complexity is unchanged; a controlled
foreground/device benchmark is still needed to quantify frame-time differences.
Portrait framing was checked on desktop hardware, not a physical mobile device.
The existing Vite warning about a minified chunk larger than 500 kB remains.
The optional high-angle screenshots are visual evidence; the five primary pairs
have the saved numeric camera/viewport measurements.
