SUP Aurora garden: short grass, hoop removal, and centered underside

Baseline: ba260e2 (completed Aurora rebalance). The island asset is also identical
to e79e488 before this pass. Destination branch: codex/aurora-crystal-garden.

The Aurora island has 1,000 deterministic short green/mint grass clumps, no upright
ornamental hoop or exclusive feet, and a mineral keel centered beneath its top.
Grass consists of two bent, tapered blades per clump. It is matte, opaque, and
batched into one InstancedMesh with shared geometry and material. Opal remains
visible between clumps. No grass texture, alpha card, animation, or shadow-casting
pass is added. Grass receives existing flower contact shadows.

The GLB and editable Blender source contain the base island. Grass is generated
in Three.js so it can shorten around the actual flower/tree positions. All clump
roots are raycast onto the imported surface; boundary clearance includes the
entire blade footprint. Pond and crystal exclusions use actual geometry. Grass
has no raycast target and is outside the flower-picking layer. Existing planting
slots are retained, including the old hoop exclusion, to avoid moving any plants.
The fixed 6,000-triangle batch remains lightweight without a density LOD or popping.

The underside is recentered after the original fracture/bevel/color construction,
preserving its 1,089 triangles, topology, corner colors, top join, and every depth
coordinate. The lowest tip moved from (-0.56, -1.23, -2.86) to approximately
(-0.04, -0.05, -2.86) in Blender coordinates. Its horizontal axis offset fell
from 1.3515 to 0.0640 units. Local fractures and small asymmetries remain. No
whole-island transform was used. The top stays at runtime Y=0.17 and diameter 8.

The generator exports from an isolated Blender process. Canonical material names
are restored there, so repeated live Blender builds cannot break the application's
name-based Aurora shading. Existing live scenes are preserved.

Performance observations:
  One isolated Chrome/SwiftShader process, sequential baseline/final/final/baseline
  blocks with matched scenes and cameras; 60-frame block medians reported.
  50 plants: baseline 308.1 / 237.0 ms; final 239.6 / 242.2 ms.
  Island only: baseline 119.8 / 132.6 ms; final 128.3 / 141.2 ms.
  Mean of block medians: 50 plants 272.55 -> 240.90 ms; empty 126.20 -> 134.75 ms.
  The first baseline block and host scheduling varied substantially. These software
  timings do not establish a GPU speedup; the later dense baseline and final are
  close. Physical Android/iOS GPU, thermal, battery, and sustained-frame performance
  remain unmeasured. The existing 50-plant renderer's high draw count remains a
  mobile concern; this pass reduces it slightly while preserving the flora renderer.
