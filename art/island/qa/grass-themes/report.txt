SOOP theme-specific grass

Shared module: e24cc39 (codex/theme-grass), based on the approved half-size grass
at 6da2b6f. Integration target: codex/garden-theme-selector. Historical Aurora,
Warm and Balanced visual branches are preserved; no merge into main.

Theme appearances:
  balanced: natural sage roots, soft mint blades, gentle yellow-green tips.
  warm: muted sage/dusty green, softer contrast and a restrained warm-white tint.
  aurora: mint and turquoise-green, restrained cool cyan reflection.
  default: neutral soft green with a subtle cool mint bias.
All palettes remain botanical green, opaque and matte. No glow, alpha cards,
new shader variants, grass animation or theme-dependent silhouette changes.

createIslandGrass accepts {theme} and the integration's {tone} alias. An explicit
theme takes precedence; absent, unknown, null and prototype-name inputs fall back
to balanced. setTheme changes the existing 10-vertex color buffer and material
uniforms in place. It preserves mesh, geometry, material, index/vertex buffers,
instance matrices/colors, clump count, placement and plant-stem cropping.

reparent(nextModel) reuses the same resources on another copy of the SAME GLB
variant. The caller must detach/reparent grass before disposing the old island.
Two bounded placement layouts cover the modern and archived asset variants;
the archived model retains hoop supports and needs their exclusion. The new
targeted foot exclusion reduces intersecting triangles from 66 to zero without
moving any grass on the modern island. No per-theme grass models are required.

Each visible grass layer remains 1,000 clumps x 6 triangles = 6,000 triangles,
one opaque main draw and zero shadow draws. It receives existing flower shadows
and remains excluded from picking. Static island totals including grass:
  Balanced / Aurora modern island: 14,099 + 6,000 = 20,099 triangles.
  Warm / Original archived island: 16,607 + 6,000 = 22,607 triangles.
Both remain below the 25,000-triangle maximum.

Focused module checks pass: all four palettes and effective botanical colors,
unknown-ID fallback, resource identity across cycles and transformed same-asset
reparenting, unchanged approved geometry/modern placement, stem cropping and
restoration, archived support exclusion, no material recompile flag, valid target
guards, and cleanup. See verify-grass-themes.mjs and module-results.json.
