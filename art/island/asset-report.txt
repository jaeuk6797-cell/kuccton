SUP floating mineral garden - color and geometry refinement
2026-11-07 (Asia/Seoul)

Baseline and scope

Working branch codex/island-refinement, starting at commit 44ce1cc.
The asymmetric outline, thin metal perimeter, main crystals, ornamental
ring and faceted underside are preserved. Work was evaluated in separate
material-only and geometry-only stages before final integration.

Diagnosis

The dullness came principally from authored mineral albedos and the dark
reflection environment. It was not a double color-space conversion bug.
The generator converts sRGB hex palettes once to linear values, writes
FLOAT_COLOR Tint attributes, and connects them directly to Principled Base
Color. Painted GLB materials have white baseColorFactor. GLTFLoader numeric
checks found zero conversion error across all 16 colored primitives.
The single 512 x 512 normal map is Non-Color/NoColorSpace. The linear scene
render receives tone mapping and sRGB display conversion once in OutputPass.

The underside does not receive shadows, ruling out shadow-map occlusion as
the cause of its darkness. Its lower normals had little direct sunlight
and reflected the dark gray-blue environment below the overhead light cards.
Hemisphere lighting also intentionally keeps the lower side dimmer.

Phase A - unchanged geometry, improved color and reflection

Ground palette changed from 4799A9 / 9373B6 / 80BABE to
8BCDD1 / B29BD4 / DBB4D3, with turquoise, lavender and pink transitions.
The mineral palette changed from 216E88 / 70509F / 54BBC0 to
5CABB9 / A28AC4 / 84D8DB. Selective edge tint changed to B9E4E2.
Rose-gold vertex colors changed from B67C96 / F0C5A5 to C38D9A / EFC6AC.
These are authored sRGB values; exported vertex colors remain linear.

Average linear vertex luminance rose from .283 to .471 on the ground and
.240 to .426 on the underside. The lower planes remain visibly darker than
the planting area because of their orientation and lighting. All 22 component
position/normal signatures and canonical oriented triangles match baseline;
the exporter only reordered some underside triangle indices.

An environment-only diagnostic on the original GLB isolated reflection fill.
The PMREM background changed from 69788B to C5D3E6, raising its linear
luminance from about .183 to .642. Existing pink, lavender, cyan and turquoise
cards, environment intensity .55, light directions/intensities and shadows
remain unchanged. The existing 256-face PMREM is captured once at startup.
There are no realtime reflection captures or new render passes.

ACES, AgX and Neutral were compared with identical camera, lights, palette
and numeric exposure 1. ACES made the upper surface too pale; AgX reduced
color separation further. Neutral retained turquoise/lavender/pink saturation,
crystal face separation and flower-petal shading. Final rendering uses
THREE.NeutralToneMapping at exposure 1. The light settings remain hemisphere
.38, main directional 1.5 and cool fill .65. No exposure increase was used.

PBR roughness, metalness, transparency and shader features are unchanged.
Ground remains MeshStandardMaterial (roughness .39, metalness .035), without
clearcoat or iridescence overhead. The smaller crystal/mineral surfaces retain
existing physical materials. Crystal vertex alpha remains about .39-.77,
roughness .055/.067/.079, and water alpha .64. No refraction or extra
transparent layers were added. Bloom remains absent.

Phase B - geometry reduction with accepted Phase A appearance

Straight fern stems use two cross-section rings instead of ten; their
positions, taper and endpoints are retained. Curved grasses use four of the
original eight rings, keeping their endpoints and luminous tips. All 240 fern
leaves are retained. The 96 small gravel fragments keep their positions,
shoulders, tips and upper facets; hidden lower detail becomes a six-face taper.
Each fragment drops from 34 to 12 triangles. The garden keeps its density.

Verification and outputs

The GLB remains Y-up with top-center origin (0,0,0). Raw X/Z dimensions are
7.931661 x 7.687877; the existing loader scales by 1.00861599 to 8 units and
places the planting origin at Y=.17. All 1,653 central radius-2.3 ray samples
hit the flat ground, with maximum deviation .00000738. Nearest permanent
decoration is at runtime radius 2.36378. All fixed fern bases are supported.
There are no static flowers, buds or petals in the exported island.

No degenerate triangles, winding conflicts, invalid normals or underside
non-manifold edges were found. The underside remains one connected shell
with its only open boundary concealed at the upper rim join. No camera,
light, backdrop or animation is exported. Actual Three.js loading preserves
materials, alpha and hierarchy. The saved .blend has one SOOP_Island scene,
SUP_Floating_Island root collection, 22 asset meshes and separate preview rig.
Its preview uses the available Khronos PBR Neutral view and matching pastel
world fill. Existing live Blender scenes were preserved.

Comparisons and functional checks

color-baseline preserves the original app views. color-diagnostics contains
the environment-only study. color-phase-a preserves matched ACES/AgX/Neutral
views and accepted material-only captures. color-final contains the final
empty, populated, rotated, rear, portrait and perimeter-detail captures.
Geometry signatures and color-space evidence are in color-baseline-geometry,
color-material-investigation, color-phase-a-validation and color-final-validation
JSON files. Controlled timing and final performance results are reported in
color-performance-comparison.json, with the raw paired run also preserved.

The actual /?view=island route was checked with and without dynamic flowers,
multiple orbit angles and a portrait viewport. Picking, orbit dragging and
new-flower growth pass; the test scene grows from 9 to 11 opened objects.
There are no runtime errors beyond the existing THREE.Clock deprecation.
Small gravel and foliage retain their visual
density at the default mobile distance. No new decoration was added.

Controlled performance observations

The same browser replayed baseline, accepted Phase A, final, final, Phase A,
baseline, measuring 60 settled frames per desktop scenario. Geometry savings
are stable: rendered triangles including shadows fall from 81,332 to 72,116
with dynamic content, and 35,436 to 26,220 for the island alone. Calls remain
405 and 26 respectively. The mock scene's dynamic content adds 379 calls,
unchanged by this task. Shader programs remain 11 and texture count remains 7.

Median frame cadence (ms)        Baseline        Phase A          Final
With dynamic content           51.1 / 54.7     91.0 / 91.9     50.5 / 88.2
Island only                    35.1 / 61.8     61.2 / 61.7     33.7 / 59.7

SwiftShader switched between fast and slow cadences even for identical
variants in this controlled session. Those results do not establish a reliable
timing speedup or hardware performance equivalence. The final portrait sample
was 72.3 ms under the slower conditions. Exported size, rendered triangles and
unchanged draw calls/material features are the reliable optimization evidence.
Real-device verification remains necessary for a physical-mobile frame budget.

Limitations

Frame cadence is measured with Chrome ANGLE/SwiftShader at DPR 1.5. It is a
software-renderer diagnostic, not physical mobile GPU timing. The Android SDK
is available but adb reports no connected device, so real Android performance
remains unverified. Thin crystal edges still show some distant-view aliasing.
Tiny gravel lower profiles are simplified and can differ in extreme close-ups.
No new material shader features or visual clutter were introduced. Real audio recording,
stereo playback and live garden synchronization were not exercised by the mock
preview route; their implementation was not changed.
