SUP floating mineral garden — visual refinement
2026-11-06

Branch codex/island-refinement starts from main 2a6f98d. The original asset
and source remain preserved in commit 0dc8638 on feat/island-art. The before
comparison uses that asset with the latest main renderer.

Visual diagnosis and changes

The first island used pale colors, constant crystal opacity, and separate
rounded underside rocks. The app lacked environment reflections and explicit
tone mapping. Its direct lighting produced harsh contrast, while the Blender
studio render compressed the pale materials toward white.

Latest main had already removed excessive flower emission and Bloom. This
revision retains its center/stamen emission of 0.15. Reduced petal whitening,
lower petal emission (0.12 to 0.04), and ACES retain center and petal color.

A single connected, tapering mineral formation replaces the rounded rocks.
Broad fracture planes, selective 0.026-unit bevels, and a sharp lower tip
create a sculpted silhouette. Turquoise, lavender, blue, and violet vary
across the formation. The thin rim and clear planting surface are preserved.

Ground colors are richer, with a subtler 512 x 512 mineral normal texture.
Glossy crystals have tinted specular highlights and vertex alpha that varies
from dense bases to clearer tips. Limited clearcoat preserves facet detail.

Four reflection cards are captured into a 256-face HDR PMREM environment
once per mount. This requires no external HDR download or per-frame reflection
capture. Warm, lavender, and cyan panels supply metallic/crystal reflections
at environment intensity 0.55. Output is sRGB with ACES exposure 1, applied
once through OutputPass. Native glTF material and texture handling is retained.

A balanced key, cool fill, and restrained hemisphere light soften contrast.
PCF shadows use intensity 0.55, radius 3, a tighter frustum, and explicit bias.
Appropriate opaque perimeter details cast shadows. Transparent crystals and
water preserve their appearance. New environment, model, and postprocessing
resources are disposed on teardown. Sound trees, growth, selection, portrait
framing, and the 1.5 DPR cap are preserved.

AgX and ACES were compared in the actual app. ACES retained richer color.
Bloom, 2x MSAA, and FXAA probes added substantial measured cost for limited
visual improvement, so the existing Bloom-free two-pass pipeline is retained.

Asset and coordinate checks

38,724 triangles; 40,637 exported vertices; 22 mesh primitives; 11 materials.
The uncompressed GLB is 2,295,192 bytes (2.30 MB / 2.19 MiB).
One embedded 512 x 512 normal PNG occupies 624,332 bytes. Other color
variation is stored in vertex colors.

Crystal COLOR_0 VEC4 alpha varies approximately 0.39–0.77. Three.js uses it
with alpha blending and material opacity 1. Crystal roughness is
0.055 / 0.067 / 0.079. Water alpha is 0.64. Materials use metallic-roughness
PBR with glTF clearcoat, iridescence, specular, and emissive-strength
extensions. No transmission/refraction pass is used.

The GLB is Y-up, with planting origin (0,0,0) and diameter 8.008182. The loader
scales it by 0.99897829 to 8 and raises it to Y=0.17, matching the flower layer.
The underside reaches local Y=-2.72. All 1,653 radius-2.3 planting samples
hit, with maximum local height deviation 0.00004318. The nearest decoration
is at runtime radius 2.3412.

There are zero degenerate triangles, winding/normal conflicts, or invalid
normals. The underside is one connected component with no non-manifold
edges. Its only boundary is concealed beneath the rim. The GLB contains no
flowers, cameras, lights, background, or animation. Preview lighting and
backdrop are confined to the editable .blend.

Measured performance

Chrome ANGLE/SwiftShader software rendering, DPR 1.5, 60 settled animation
frames per scenario. Frame interval includes software GPU work and host
scheduling. It is not hardware mobile GPU timing. Full measurements and
viewport details are in qa/performance-results.json.

Scenario                  Calls before/final    Median frame interval
Desktop with flowers          428 / 405           53.2 / 56.1 ms
Island only                    49 / 26            44.5 / 38.6 ms
Portrait with flowers         428 / 405           44.9 / 47.9 ms

Island-only interval improved 13.3%. Full-scene interval rose 5.5% and
portrait rose 6.7% with richer reflection shading. Full-scene drawn triangles,
including shadows, fell from 137,925 to 98,795. Median CPU submission changed
from 1.0 to 1.9 ms with flowers and from 0.4 to 0.6 ms for the island alone.

Subtle Bloom increased final desktop interval from 56.1 to 82.4 ms.
2x MSAA increased it to 131.4 ms. A later paired FXAA probe changed
104.5 to 152.8 ms under a slower host workload. That probe is excluded from
the primary comparison. Physical Android/iOS performance remains unverified.

Remaining differences

The result is more restrained and spacious than the reference. Alpha,
facet tint, and reflected light approximate crystal optical depth. The
underside uses broad stylized fracture planes. Thin reflective edges retain
some aliasing at distant zoom. The app's procedural flowers and sound trees
remain. Dense static flora, butterflies, and complex refractive effects from
the reference are outside the model.

Source and reproduction

public/models/island.glb is the runtime asset. art/island/island.blend is
the editable scene. art/island/generate_island.py contains the fixed-seed
geometry and materials. With Blender 5.2, run

  blender --background --factory-startup --python art/island/generate_island.py

Append -- --render to regenerate preview.png at 96 denoised Cycles samples.
The generator preserves interactive scenes and saves the new scene through
a separate Blender process. The prior source remains in commit 0dc8638.
