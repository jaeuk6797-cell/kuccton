Starting point

Focused revision of commit 266c127 on codex/island-refinement. Earlier source,

Geometry

A fifteen-shoulder asymmetric outline with lightly dressed corners replaces
the circular boundary. The planting surface, metal seam and mineral shell
share the outline. Long planar stretches keep the contour intentional.
The rose-gold band is roughly half its old depth, varies gently in depth,
and has one champagne upper seam. The rear resonator is subtly asymmetric.

The underside is a single connected formation with broad fracture planes,
0.013-unit edge chamfers and an offset suspended tip at local Y=-2.86.
The tip extends toward the front, making the tapered silhouette visible from
the app camera. No hanging rock clusters or extra ornaments were introduced.
Surface sampling, leaf tessellation, gravel quantity and trim sections were
reduced while retaining the major crystal silhouettes and clear center.

Materials and rendering

Ground metallic is reduced from .09 to .035, with roughness .39. It uses
standard metallic-roughness reflection. A ground clearcoat/iridescence probe
had negligible visual benefit and higher measured cost, so it was removed.
Mineral metallic is .12, roughness .32, and clearcoat .22/.24. Broader tinted
reflections read across the surfaces without a mirror-like planting area.

The app captures broad pink, lavender, cyan and turquoise reflection cards
into its existing 256-face environment. Lower peak radiance and a one-time
0.14-radian PMREM blur soften the sky reflections. Environment intensity .55,
ACES exposure 1, existing postprocessing and shadow setup remain. Cool-fill
intensity .65 improves underside visibility. There are no additional lights,
per-frame reflection captures, refraction passes or postprocessing passes.

Crystal geometry and layered facet alpha remain sharp and glossy, with
roughness .055/.067/.079 and vertex alpha approximately .39-.77. Water alpha
is .64. Materials use export-compatible glTF PBR, clearcoat, iridescence,
specular and IOR properties. Transparency is simple single-sided blending.

Integration and geometry validation

The exported asset is Y-up with top-center origin (0,0,0). Raw X/Z dimensions
are 7.931661 x 7.687877. The unchanged loader scales by 1.00861599 to an
8-unit maximum diameter and raises the planting origin to world Y=.17.

All 1,653 sampled points within world radius 2.3 hit the planting surface.
Maximum height deviation is .00000738. The nearest permanent decoration is
at world radius 2.36378; triangle-based clearance also passes. All eight
fixed fern bases are on the surface. No static flowers, buds or petals exist.

The GLB has zero degenerate triangles, winding conflicts or invalid normals.
The underside is one connected component without non-manifold edges. Its
79 boundary edges are confined to the concealed upper join beneath the rim.
Cameras, lights, backdrop and animations are absent from the GLB.

App verification and performance

Final app verification and measured performance are recorded in the paired
shape-before/shape-after captures and shape-performance-comparison.json.
The actual /?view=island route was checked with and without flowers, rotated
views and a mobile-like viewport. Flower picking, orbit dragging and new
flower growth pass. No loading failures were observed. The existing
THREE.Clock deprecation warning remains.

Render calls remain 405 with flowers and 26 for the island only. Rendered
triangles including shadows fall from 98,795 to 81,332 with flowers and
52,899 to 35,436 for the island only. No extra render pass was added.

Host load varied substantially between early and late measurements. A
controlled same-session ABBA replay measured the baseline at 101.0/100.7 ms
with flowers and 72.7/72.7 ms empty, versus 102.8/102.6 and 73.6/72.9 ms
for the temporary coated ground. Removing that ground coating measured
91.0 ms with flowers and 61.5 ms empty, about 11%/16% lower than the coated
variant. The final export uses that simpler ground material. These paired
figures are diagnostic software-renderer observations, not mobile FPS claims.

The final exported GLB was loaded again and checked at 52.3 ms with flowers,
34.5 ms empty and 45.1 ms in portrait. Earlier baseline runs were 56.2,
39.0 and 48.0 ms respectively. Use the controlled pairing above to assess
material cost; these separate final/baseline runs experienced different host
load and are included for traceability rather than a guaranteed speedup.

Timing uses Chrome ANGLE/SwiftShader software rendering, DPR 1.5 and 60
settled animation frames. It is not physical Android/iOS GPU timing. Thin
reflective edges retain some aliasing at distant zoom. Crystal depth uses
facet color, alpha and reflection rather than optical refraction. The
underside deliberately retains darker mineral values beneath the bright
planting surface. No audio, authentication or database behavior changed.
