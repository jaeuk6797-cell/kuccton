Visual changes
Aurora now uses a brighter rose-pink, lavender and aqua sky, stronger diffuse
illumination and softer shadows. The underside has brighter turquoise and
lavender mineral colors. Flower/crystal highlights retain selective luminous
edges and warm centers; wisps and the existing 140 particles stay secondary.

The ground's broad coated highlight is substantially reduced: roughness rises
from 0.14 to 0.42, clearcoat falls from 0.70 to 0.18, and the broad upper-view
specular multiplier falls from 31.0 to 1.2. New derivative-filtered mineral
grains and small view-dependent glints replace that broad white shine, while
pink/aqua opal color shifts remain. Matched island-only screenshots show a
more granular, colored crystal surface instead of the former slick pale plate.
Glow strength drops from 0.38 to 0.24 and its threshold rises from 0.85 to 1.05.
The reference is retained as reference.png for direct mood/palette inspection.

Performance limits and remaining issues
GPU timers are available in updated captures, but browser focus changes and
concurrent tabs made timings highly noisy. Updated mean GPU samples range from
7.43 to 14.36 ms across views; sample counts vary from 29 to 180. These numbers
are observations only. Dark/Warm comparison captures lack matched GPU queries,
and frame gaps reach about one second, so no controlled FPS or GPU-regression
claim is justified. A focused single-tab/device benchmark remains outstanding.
The existing Vite warning about a minified chunk over 500 kB remains. Portrait
was checked on desktop hardware rather than a physical phone. No functional
failure was observed in the completed build, preservation and interaction checks.
